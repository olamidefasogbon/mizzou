Frequently Asked Questions

In case of doubt, please review carefully the following questions as we will not answer inquiries if your question is covered here.

« Will I receive a fellowship if selected to participate in the program? »

Yes. Accepted students will receive a monthly living allowance of 1800 CHF per month (host labs located in Lausanne, Sion and Fribourg) or 2500 CHF per month (host labs located in Geneva and Neuchâtel) (pro rata to the actual duration) to cover their housing and living expenses. In addition, travel costs will be refunded on request, upon approval by the host lab.

« What are the duration and dates of the summer internship? »

The E3 internship can last a minimum of 2 months and a maximum of 3 months. The dates of the internship are discussed during the recruitment phase that takes place after the application period (i.e., from mid November till beginning of March). If your application is of interest to one or more laboratories, the relevant laboratory(ies) will contact you to arrange an interview. During the interview, you will discuss the start and end dates of your internship with the laboratory. Internships may begin between May and October, but cannot start before May 1. If the internship is recognized by your home university as part of the study plan (i.e., you will get ECTS credits for it) and if the host professor agrees, the internship duration can be longer than 3 months. If you are interested in this possibility, you must discuss this with the potential host professor(s) during the interviews.

« I am currently enrolled in the second year of my Bachelor degree and I will complete my second year of Bachelor before summer. Am I eligible for the E3 program? »

Unfortunately not. To be eligible, you must have completed at least your first two years of Bachelor at the time of the application, i.e., you have to provide the transcripts and GPA of your two years of Bachelor in the application form.

« I will complete my Master before the summer break. Am I eligible for the E3 program? »

To participate in the E3 program, you must have a student status at your home university (i.e., you must be enrolled in a Bachelor or a Master program) during the entire duration of the summer internship. The start and end dates of the internship are agreed upon by you and the host laboratory during the selection process. If you are uncertain about your graduation date, you may still apply, provided that you have a student status at the time of the application.

« I am a PhD student. Is there any way to come to EPFL? »

Yes, certainly, but not through the E3 program. You need to organize your internship yourself, and for that, you may want to contact researchers or laboratories directly. If you choose to do so, be sure to provide sufficient detail about your background and your specific interests in the laboratory’s research. Vague or generic letters are unlikely to be successful.

« Will you help with accommodation? Will I receive accommodation from EPFL? »

You will be responsible for finding your own accommodation. However, if you are offered a fellowship, the administrative staff of your host laboratory will almost invariably provide you with suggestions and ressources to help you find accomodation, such as student forums, student residences, …

« Is an English certificate (IELTS, TOEFL, etc) required when applying? »

English language certificates are not required but, if you have any, you can upload them as additional documents when applying. Most of the laboratories at EPFL use English to do research and for everyday life. French is the main language outside the campus.

« Can I see the list of projects? Can I choose my project before applying? »

There is no list of predefined projects and therefore there is no way to choose a project in advance. Projects are proposed by the recruiting laboratories directly to selected candidates and can be discussed to find the best match between your research interests and the laboratory goals. You should choose carefully your scientific interests on the application portal, because this will be important information for the laboratories in the selection process. Please also list up to three preferred research laboratories where you would like to do your internship. Take the necessary time to identify the right laboratories in order to increase your chances of acceptance. An online search tool to find specific laboratories is provided here.

« What is the GPA? »

The GPA (grade point average) is the average of your diploma grades expressed as a cardinal number.

« On the application form, shall I mention the GPA in a specific format? »

Specify your cumulative GPA at the time of application exactly in the format provided by your school on your transcripts, without attempting to rescale it to 0-100 or 0-4 or adapt it to any specific system.

« On the application form, shall I quote my Bachelor or my Master institution? And mention the Bachelor or the Master GPA? »

When you apply on the web, complete the application form with the details of your current home institution and current program (BSc or MSc) when completing the fields « Current program », « Current Institution », « Current official GPA », etc. If you have no Master GPA yet, write NA under « Current official GPA ». Additionally, use the fields « Previous program », « Previous institution », « Previous GPA », etc. to include your Bachelor data. In any case, upload all available transcripts.

« Shall I use a specific format for my CV? »

You can submit your CV in any layout, as long as it is a PDF file (max 5 MB).

« I have just started the first semester of my Master, and the first exams will take place after the application deadline. Therefore, I cannot specify the transcripts and a GPA for the first year of Master when applying: is this a problem? »

This is not a problem. Specify the GPA of your Bachelor and provide the transcripts of your Bachelor. Use the comment fields in the application form to clarify that you have just started the first year of Master and do not have any grades yet for it at the time of the application.

« In the application form, I am not sure which preferred professors/laboratories to select; what shall I do? »

You are asked to select up to three research domains you would like to do research in and you can specify (not compulsory) up to three preferred research laboratories you would like to work with. Take the necessary time to identify the right laboratories in order to increase your chances of acceptance. An online search tool to find specific labs is provided here. If you are not sure about your preferred host laboratories, you do not need to specify any.

« What documents shall I send with my application? »

Your CV, your complete transcripts, your statement of purpose (i.e., research statement/motivation letter), and a scan of your passport or national ID document (if you do not have a passport yet). Transcripts can be in any form your university provides them and should cover the whole of your academic career (Bachelor and Master so far). If you only have them in paper copy, please scan them. If they are not in English, please send both the originals and a translation (no need for the translation to be official, you can do it yourself). There is no strict page limit for the statement of purpose (i.e., research statement/motivation letter) but we recommend to target maximum two pages. The only format we support for all files is PDF.

« I do not have a passport yet and my national ID is not in English. Is the scan of my national ID ok to apply to E3? »

Yes, if you do not have a passport, please upload a scan of your national ID on the application portal. No problem if this document is not in English.

« Are the “statement of purpose” and the “research statement” or “motivation letter” the same thing? »

Yes. In the statement of purpose, you can explain why you would like to participate to the E3 Program. If you have any target host laboratories, you can explain there your choice.

« I have recommendation letters; can I send them? »

Yes, we consider recommendation letters for this program, but they are not mandatory. We do not provide a separate platform to send recommendation letters and they simply have to be uploaded as an additional attachment. If this is an issue for your referee, not uploading any recommendation letter will not have any negative impact on the evaluation of your file.

« I am late, my school is on holidays, official transcripts take time to process, my latest grades will be known only a few days after the deadline; can I have an additional delay to apply? »

No, whatever the reason. If the application portal is closed, we are sorry but it is too late and we cannot do anything for you. If you completed the application form but could not upload the documents on time, the same applies: too late.

« How will I know if I am accepted for the program? »

After the application dealine, selected candidates may be invited to online interviews by one or more host laboratories. Following a successful interview, you may receive an internship offer from a faculty member within approx. two months of the application deadline. Around mid-March, if you have not received an internship offer from a host laboratory, you will receive an email informing you that the selection process has been closed. Please do not email us to inquire about the status of your application. We receive a large number of applications and are therefore unable to respond to individual inquiries.

« Shall I expect issues with the immigration visa? »

If you are accepted into the program, EPFL will send you detailed instructions to start the visa procedure. In some cases, the visa is directly requested by EPFL. Unfortunately, we cannot anticipate issues which could impact the delivery of visa by the authorities: the delivery of visas depends only on the competent authorities. Nevertheless, we will provide suggestions and help in case of issues and delays.

« Will I get any support for the visa request if needed? »

Yes, if you are enrolled in the program you will get assistance and instructions to file your visa request. Some embassies request the payment of fees when retrieving the visa: this will be at the student’s charge.

« Do I need to have a valid passport to apply? »

You are requested to upload a scan of your passport or national ID document (if you do not have a passport) when you apply. If you are a non-EU/EFTA citizen, when offered a position by a lab, you will need to provide the scan of a valid passport. To obtain a visa, your passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the end date of your internship.

Are there uncertainties linked to the current geopolitical situation?

Due to the current geopolitical situation and uncertainty surrounding its evolution, including the possibility of further deterioration, EPFL reserves the right to modify the participation conditions of the E3 program or, if necessary to cancel the program. EPFL cannot be held liable should this be the case.

Are EPFL students eligible for the E3 program?

No. The E3 program is intended exclusively for students from other institutions. EPFL offers a dedicated program for its own students, called Summer in the Lab. In addition, EPFL students can contact faculty members directly to inquire about summer internship opportunities in their laboratories.
