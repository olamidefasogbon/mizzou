Your Application
A strong application is your first step toward a fulfilling career. We encourage you to take your time to find the right opportunity that aligns with your qualifications, experience, and motivation. A thoughtful and detailed application that highlights your strengths will ensure that we can fully assess your potential. Our team is committed to reviewing each application with care and attention to detail.
Selection & Hiring
If your profile is a match, you’ll be invited to the next stage, which could include interviews or technical assessments. Our goal is to find the best fit for both you and CERN. If selected, we’ll be with you every step of the way during the administrative process, answering all your questions.
Your Arrival at CERN
Once the hiring is finalized, we’ll guide you through the onboarding process, providing all the necessary information to help you settle in quickly and comfortably. From team introductions to practical details, we’re here to make your transition smooth and enjoyable. We are excited to have you join us and look forward to supporting you as you begin your journey with CERN.
